# ACQ-001 — Public Positioning and Entry CTAs

## Purpose

The public RFxchange experience must tell a first-time visitor what the platform is, how it differs from familiar but incomplete categories, and where to begin.

ACQ-001 does not require paid enrollment to be open. It requires the public product surface to explain the RFxchange honestly and provide clear `Join` and `See How It Works` entry points while launch readiness remains explicit.

## Public thesis

The RFxchange is presented as a local business growth network where organizations can become discoverable by capability, find relevant demand, form useful connections, access support, and follow activity toward reported outcomes.

The landing page carries the shared product spine:

- **Visible** — capabilities become discoverable.
- **Connected** — demand meets a useful next step.
- **Actionable** — connections move into real workflows.

## Category differentiation

The public experience explicitly distinguishes the product from three narrower categories:

### More than a directory

A directory primarily establishes who exists. RFxchange organization profiles are positioned as inputs to capability discovery and connected opportunity, referral, teaming, and resource workflows.

### Not a social feed

The network is organized around organizations, capabilities, opportunities, referrals, resources, and business relationships rather than followers or general-purpose posting.

### Broader than a bid portal

RFx workflows are one part of a connected business-development environment that also includes discovery, partnerships, referrals, resources, and market context.

These statements describe the product model without claiming guaranteed leads, awards, financing, revenue, or other business outcomes.

## Entry CTAs

The hero exposes two primary actions:

- `Join` → the public founding/join section.
- `See How It Works` → the connected-journey section.

The Join surface remains transparent that production enrollment opens only after the account, legal, geography, and commercial controls required for production enrollment are ready. ACQ-001 therefore does not imply that checkout or unrestricted registration is live.

## Visual behavior

The differentiation surface reuses the existing warm-ivory public composition and typography. It is rendered as three simple text columns with a restrained gold rule rather than additional cards or dashboard containers, preserving the uncluttered presentation system.

On narrower screens the columns collapse into a single reading sequence.

## Acceptance

ACQ-001 is complete when production CI proves that:

- the public page explains the RFxchange purpose;
- the page explicitly differentiates the RFxchange from a directory, social feed, and bid portal;
- `Join` and `See How It Works` are visible and target their corresponding landing-page sections;
- prohibited guaranteed-outcome claims remain absent;
- the production application still typechecks, lints, and builds successfully.
