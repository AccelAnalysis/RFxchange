# Post-Wave 3 public marketing surfaces

**Status:** implementation evidence for the permanent homepage and Founding campaign surface

**Feature-ID effect:** none

## Purpose

This implementation follows the accepted Wave 3 Network closeout at `784f2668f3fe952076d83c4a4fa1886be6d98357` and executes issue #136 without beginning Wave 4 or commercial-domain implementation.

It establishes two distinct public jobs:

- the permanent homepage explains the durable RFxchange customer value proposition; and
- `/founding` converts appropriate early organizations into launch-cohort participation without redefining the product around the temporary campaign.

## Customer-value architecture

Both surfaces consume the reconciled message architecture:

```text
Understandable → Discoverable → Connectable → Actionable
```

The permanent homepage leads with the outcome:

> Make your business easier to understand—and easier to do business with.

It explains the business-language translation gap, AMACS by customer purpose, ordinary-language entry, non-authoritative interpretation assistance, participant confirmation, live Network discovery/connections/resources/education, and the boundary between current Network capability and later RFx/credibility/commercial domains.

## Truthful availability

A shared localized availability component prevents the homepage and Founding page from drifting. It identifies as live only the accepted Wave 3 capabilities:

- organization activation, market profile and authorized enrichment;
- capability/geography/service-area discovery;
- organization-owned referrals and external-recipient continuity;
- approved resource-provider discovery and consented connections;
- provider resources; and
- persistent Quick Start, role paths and contextual explainers.

It identifies RFx Core, evaluation, credibility, outcomes, advanced capacity routing and commercial entitlements as later governed pathways.

## Founding commercial boundary

The Founding page states the approved planned model of `$49/month` and a 250-organization cap while making clear that paid enrollment does not open until the approved entitlement and billing experience are operational.

Founding recognition remains separate from verification, qualification, credibility, neutral discovery and business outcomes. No billing, Stripe, entitlement, ranking or credibility implementation is added here.

## Internationalization and accessibility

All new RFxchange-controlled page content uses a dedicated `marketing-pages` namespace in English, Spanish, French, Italian and German. The existing participant-authored-content translation exclusions remain unchanged.

The pages use semantic sections, ordered process steps, native FAQ disclosure controls, visible focus treatment, responsive layouts, reduced-transparency fallbacks and descriptive image alternatives. Stock photography is explicitly identified as atmosphere rather than live market evidence.
