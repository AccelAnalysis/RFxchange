# Slice 3.8 — Persistent Network Education

**Status: PLANNING BRIEF ONLY — DO NOT IMPLEMENT UNTIL EXPLICITLY AUTHORIZED**

- `EDU-016` — Reusable Quick Start and role paths

## Objective

Turn Wave 2 orientation into a persistent education system that remains useful after onboarding, explains live Wave 3 behaviors in context, and teaches the RFxchange value model without pretending future RFx, credibility or commercial functionality is active.

Persistent education must help participants understand not only how to use a control, but why the underlying information or workflow matters to the market. The customer-facing learning spine is:

```text
Understandable → Discoverable → Connectable → Actionable
```

- **Understandable** — the organization can express what it does, where it operates and what market context matters in terms the Exchange can structure and reuse.
- **Discoverable** — confirmed capability, geography, service-area and other authorized context make the organization easier to find for relevant reasons.
- **Connectable** — the reason for a connection can travel into referrals, provider relationships and later teaming/RFx activity instead of collapsing into a name and contact record.
- **Actionable** — released workflows convert relevant context into an appropriate next action while preserving the distinction between potential fit, connection, decision and outcome.

The experience must reinforce the approved brand promise **Be found. Find opportunity. Build the connection.** without turning education into promotional copy.

## Education value architecture

Persistent education must progressively teach the following customer value propositions when they are relevant to the participant's current action.

### Make the organization understandable

Profile enrichment is not administrative completion for its own sake. Participants should understand that confirmed capability and market-context information creates a reusable business-development position that helps the Exchange understand when the organization may be relevant to a market need.

Preferred explanatory pattern:

> **Capabilities describe what your organization can actually do.**
> Confirmed capability information helps other participants discover your organization for relevant market needs.

Do not imply that a self-reported capability is verified, qualified or sufficient for a particular opportunity.

### Start in ordinary business language

Participants should not be required to understand AMACS terminology or procurement vocabulary before receiving value. Where interpretation assistance is authorized, education should make clear that the participant may begin with the business language they already know.

Preferred explanatory pattern:

> **Start with what you know. The RFxchange can help organize it. You confirm what is true.**

### Explain AMACS by customer purpose first

AMACS is not introduced primarily as a taxonomy, code catalog or compliance burden. Where relevant, explain it first as the governed common language beneath the Exchange:

> **AMACS gives the Exchange a common way to connect what the market needs with what organizations can actually do.**

Progressive education may explain the broader relationship:

```text
Need → Capability → Evidence → Response → Decision → Outcome
```

Only teach the portions that are useful to the participant's current live workflow. Future RFx, credibility and outcome domains must remain explicitly identified as later product pathways until authorized and released.

### Explain AI and human authority clearly

Where AI/interpretation assistance is available, education must explain the boundary rather than marketing generic “AI-powered” functionality.

Participants should understand that:

- ordinary language is an acceptable starting point;
- assistance may propose relevant AMACS concepts or structured interpretations;
- the suggestion is non-authoritative;
- an authorized participant decides whether it accurately represents the organization or market need;
- acceptance still requires the applicable separate server-authorized domain write;
- AI does not establish verification, qualification, credibility, provider approval or outcome truth; and
- already-structured matching remains deterministic and does not require an LLM.

Preferred concise pattern:

> **AI can suggest. People confirm.**

### Explain connected workflows rather than isolated modules

Education should show why Network activities relate to one another. Discovery, referrals, resource routing and later teaming/RFx activity should not be taught as unrelated product modules when the shared context can explain the relationship.

Examples:

- organization capability → relevant discovery;
- identified business need → relevant provider/resource context;
- organization discovery → structured referral with purpose;
- capability gap → later teammate/resource pathway where authorized;
- confirmed market context → later RFx requirements, response, decision and outcome pathways.

Education must never represent a referral, recommendation, connection, potential match or AI suggestion as a decision or successful outcome.

## Product rules

### `EDU-016`

Provide reusable post-onboarding learning paths including Quick Start plus Business, Issuer and Resource Provider paths. Role paths are educational and may be shown according to organization roles/objectives, but they do not grant permissions or status.

Content links to live workflows where available and clearly identifies functionality reserved for later waves rather than pretending it is active.

The reusable Quick Start should teach the value progression before or alongside navigation mechanics:

1. **Make your business understandable** — confirm what the organization does, where it operates and the market context that matters.
2. **See where you fit** — explore permitted organizations and capabilities using structured market information.
3. **Build useful connections** — carry the reason for the connection into referrals and other released Network workflows.
4. **Find support when you need it** — discover approved resource providers in the context of the business need as those workflows are released.
5. **Grow with the Exchange** — explain that the same market position can support later teaming, RFx, evidence, decision and outcome workflows without depicting them as live before authorization.

Quick Start should remain concise, resumable and action-oriented. It is not a marketing carousel and must not delay a participant who already understands the available workflow.

Business-path education should emphasize organization capability, discoverability, relevant connections and profile reuse.

Issuer-path education may explain the participant's ability to express a future market need in ordinary language and the later Need → Capability relationship, but it must not implement or simulate Wave 4 RFx creation before authorization.

Resource Provider education should explain official-provider approval, service context, territory, eligibility, intake and the distinction between provider discovery/recommendation and actual provider acceptance or service outcome.

### `EDU-017`

Major workflows answer four practical questions at the point of action:

1. What is this?
2. Why does it matter here?
3. What happens if I do this?
4. What happens next?

The added **Why does it matter here?** question should connect the control to the user's economic objective rather than merely describing interface mechanics.

Explain consequential actions before commitment, including referrals, provider connections, organization/profile changes and other live Network actions established in Wave 3.

Representative patterns include:

#### Capability/profile context

> **Capabilities describe what your organization can actually do.**
> Confirming them helps the Exchange understand when your organization may be relevant to a market need.

#### Interpretation assistance

> **Describe it in your own words.**
> The RFxchange can suggest relevant AMACS concepts. You decide which ones accurately represent your organization.

#### Discovery

> **Search by what you need—not only by business name or industry.**
> Results use authorized capability and geography context; a potential match is not qualification or endorsement.

#### Referral

> **A useful referral carries the reason for the connection, not just contact information.**
> Share only the minimum necessary context and preserve consent throughout the referral lifecycle.

#### Resource provider

> **Resources appear in the context of the need they may help address.**
> Official provider status identifies an approved provider role; it does not guarantee fit, eligibility, capacity, availability, acceptance or outcome.

These examples are messaging patterns, not mandatory verbatim UI copy. Final strings must reflect the exact live domain state and localization requirements.

## Brand and education rules

- Education uses the direct, calm and specific Content and Messaging System.
- Education explains customer purpose before internal architecture or taxonomy terminology.
- Reusable education must not become a generic dashboard, promotional carousel or modal wall.
- High-value explainers appear at the point of consequence, remain dismissible/resumable as policy permits and do not repeat unnecessarily.
- Explain the minimum useful concept in context; deeper detail should be progressively disclosed rather than forcing every participant through a long tutorial.
- Visual examples use real authorized product state or clearly labeled synthetic/tutorial content isolated from live records and analytics.
- Education may describe future concepts only when it clearly states that the workflow is not currently available.
- Role paths do not imply organization authority, Official Resource Provider approval, verification, credibility or paid entitlement.
- Founding or other commercial recognition may be explained only as historical/commercial participation and must not imply preferred discovery, qualification, verification, credibility or better AI interpretation.
- Motion is restrained and reduced-motion compatible; production sound/haptics remain outside this slice.
- Empty and unavailable-path messages preserve intent only when the product actually persists that intent.
- Education content must use the existing localization path. Participant-authored business descriptions remain outside automatic interface translation according to the i18n authority.

## Acceptance intent

- users can reopen Quick Start and the appropriate role paths after onboarding;
- Quick Start teaches the Understandable → Discoverable → Connectable → Actionable value progression without becoming promotional or blocking;
- profile/capability education explains why confirmed capability information matters to discovery without implying verification or qualification;
- where interpretation assistance appears, education accurately explains ordinary-language entry, AMACS structure, non-authoritative AI suggestions and participant confirmation;
- workflow explainers answer what the object/action is, why it matters in the current business context, what committing the action does and what happens next;
- referrals and provider connections explain context/consent/authority boundaries and do not imply successful outcomes;
- education reflects actual live behavior and legal/privacy boundaries;
- education state is separate from authorization and cannot unlock features;
- mobile/desktop experiences remain accessible and uncluttered;
- all education strings follow the existing localization architecture;
- synthetic examples are visibly labeled, deterministic and isolated from live records/analytics;
- future Wave 4/5/6 functionality is not represented as live.

## Expected implementation qualities

Versionable content/config where practical, deep links to the relevant live workflow, role/objective-aware presentation without authorization coupling, progressive disclosure, analytics hooks only where already authorized, shared educational primitives and tests proving education cannot grant access or mutate domain state.

The content model should make value-proposition language centrally maintainable enough that Quick Start, contextual explainers and public marketing can remain semantically aligned without forcing identical wording across every surface.

## Explicit non-scope

Do not implement Wave 4 first-RFx education (`EDU-011`–`EDU-013`), endorsement education (`EDU-015`), paid training products, certification, automatic user qualification, Intelligence Dark, Presentation Mode, production sound or haptics.

This planning reconciliation does not authorize Slice 3.8 implementation early, does not complete either Feature ID and does not expand Wave 3 domain scope.

## Exit checkpoint

Participants can learn and relearn the live Network in context after onboarding, understand why their structured market information matters, and understand consequential workflow effects before committing actions.

A participant should be able to articulate the core experience in practical terms: make the organization understandable, become discoverable for relevant reasons, carry useful context into connections, and use released workflows to take the next appropriate action.
