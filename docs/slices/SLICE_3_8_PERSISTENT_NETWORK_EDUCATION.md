## Product rules

### `EDU-016`

Provide reusable post-onboarding learning paths including Quick Start plus Business, Issuer and Resource Provider paths. Role paths are educational and may be shown according to organization roles/objectives, but they do not grant permissions or status.

Content links to live workflows where available and clearly identifies functionality reserved for later waves rather than pretending it is active.

### `EDU-017`

Major workflows answer three practical questions at the point of action:

1. What is this?
2. What happens if I do this?
3. What happens next?

Explain consequential actions before commitment, including referrals, provider connections, organization/profile changes and other live Network actions established in Wave 3.

## Brand and education rules

- Education uses the direct, calm and specific Content and Messaging System.
- Reusable education must not become a generic dashboard, promotional carousel or modal wall.
- High-value explainers appear at the point of consequence, remain dismissible/resumable as policy permits and do not repeat unnecessarily.
- Visual examples use real authorized product state or clearly labeled synthetic/tutorial content isolated from live records and analytics.
- Education may describe future concepts only when it clearly states that the workflow is not currently available.
- Role paths do not imply organization authority, Official Resource Provider approval, verification, credibility or paid entitlement.
- Motion is restrained and reduced-motion compatible; production sound/haptics remain outside this slice.
- Empty and unavailable-path messages preserve intent only when the product actually persists that intent.

## Acceptance intent

- users can reopen Quick Start and the appropriate role paths after onboarding;
- workflow explainers appear at high-value/consequential Network actions without becoming mandatory repetitive modal friction;
- education reflects actual live behavior and legal/privacy boundaries;
- education state is separate from authorization and cannot unlock features;
- mobile/desktop experiences remain accessible and uncluttered;
- synthetic examples are visibly labeled, deterministic and isolated from live records/analytics;
- future Wave 4/5/6 functionality is not represented as live.

## Expected implementation qualities

Versionable content/config where practical, deep links to the relevant live workflow, role/objective-aware presentation without authorization coupling, analytics hooks only where already authorized, shared educational primitives and tests proving education cannot grant access or mutate domain state.

## Explicit non-scope

Do not implement Wave 4 first-RFx education (`EDU-011`–`EDU-013`), endorsement education (`EDU-015`), paid training products, certification, automatic user qualification, Intelligence Dark, Presentation Mode, production sound or haptics.

## Exit checkpoint

Participants can learn and relearn the live Network in context after onboarding, and consequential workflows explain their effects before users commit actions.

## Completion discipline

After merge, reconcile evidence for all 38 Network features and verify the Wave 3 exit condition before RFx Core convergence or Wave 4 implementation begins.
