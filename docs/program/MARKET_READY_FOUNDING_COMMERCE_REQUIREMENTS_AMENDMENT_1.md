# Market-Ready Founding Commerce — Requirements Amendment 1

This amendment adds one immutable packet-local requirement to `WP-MARKET-READY-FOUNDING-COMMERCE-01`. Existing MRFC requirements and unresolved `COM-039` through `COM-041` remain unchanged.

## `MRFC-LIFECYCLE-001` — Membership state follows authoritative subscription lifecycle

RFxchange must keep Founding membership recognition synchronized with authenticated provider lifecycle evidence for the exact organization subscription. When authoritative evidence no longer supports the recognized active subscription state, RFxchange must remove active Founding recognition and any benefits that depend on that active commercial state. Duplicate or out-of-order evidence must not restore stale state. Later authoritative evidence may restore recognition only through the same governed reconciliation boundary.

Acceptance types: functional, domain-security, governance.

Acceptance evidence must include negative lifecycle transitions represented by the adopted subscription model, including delinquent, canceled or inactive-equivalent states, and must prove correct organization correlation, idempotency, ordering safety and later re-entry truthfulness.

A successful initial enrollment alone does not satisfy this requirement.
