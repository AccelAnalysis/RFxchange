## Exchange Light cartography

The shared cartography contract defines:

- Mapbox Standard with day/faded presentation, subdued transit and retained 3D objects;
- warm low-saturation land and spatial fallbacks;
- selected-locality gold field and restrained black/gold boundary pair;
- nonfocus geography mask;
- low-contrast building intent;
- progressive detail thresholds;
- proprietary blue-to-gold density-gradient stops;
- protected focal-target dimensions;
- organization-node and subordinate-location geometry.

The controlled locality model consumes this contract so SVG/reference and Mapbox surfaces share the same selected and surrounding locality meaning.

## Organization nodes

The generic teardrop pin is removed from the Mapbox point-overlay styling. The map now uses:

- a graphite organization node with RF glyph, gold ring and restrained halo;
- blue candidate state;
- green confirmed-location state only where confirmation is authoritative;
- a smaller ivory/dark-gold satellite contract for additional organization locations.

The persistent ExchangeSpatialScene organization marker already used the proprietary layered node architecture and retains its halo, core, RF glyph and label layers.

## Domain availability

The cartography contract defines future visual meanings without rendering them as live:

- opportunity beacons require an authoritative published opportunity projection;
- service fields require provider service-territory authority;
- relationship paths require authoritative events;
- credibility seals and outcome paths require evidence;
- synthetic or planned objects cannot appear as live;
- non-spatial objects cannot receive decorative point geography.

No live opportunity, provider, referral, RFx, credibility or outcome layer is introduced by B3.
