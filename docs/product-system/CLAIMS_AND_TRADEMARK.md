# Claims and Trademark Discipline

## Preferred product language

- connective economic infrastructure
- capability-based discovery
- qualified routing
- complete business journey
- measured outcomes
- map-based business growth network

## Claims that require evidence or qualification

Revenue, jobs, financing, contract awards, provider efficiency, procurement equity, and ROI are outcomes to measure—not automatic consequences of platform participation.

Public copy must not promise guaranteed leads, guaranteed contracts, guaranteed financing, predetermined ROI, or replacement of institutional systems of record.

## Measurement language

Use the ladder:

**Participation → Discovery → Connection → Activity → Outcome → Verified impact**

Do not collapse one stage into another. A profile view is not a lead. A connection is not a sale. A proposal is not an award.

## Trademark

Use **The RFxchange™** and the `™` mark in branded wordmarks and formal first references. Do not use `®` until counsel confirms registration and product governance records that approval.
