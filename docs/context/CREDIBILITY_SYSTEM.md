# RFxchange Credibility System Context

## Core principle

Credibility belongs to the **organization**, not individual users. The MVP does not expose a public numerical trust score.

Credibility is communicated through badges backed by auditable credibility events.

## Five credibility families

1. **Verified** — independently established organizational facts.
2. **Active** — meaningful platform participation and contribution.
3. **Experienced** — accumulated RFx, transaction and completed-interaction history.
4. **Trusted** — behavior demonstrated through qualifying interactions and standardized counterparty confirmation.
5. **Endorsed** — broad organizational endorsement issued by an authorized Verified organization.

Recognition markers such as Founding Organization are separate and do not establish substantive credibility.

## Evidence rule

No substantive badge is awarded merely because an organization makes an unsupported claim.

An AI or other interpretation candidate is not a credibility event, capability assertion, evidence record or verification. Accepting a suggestion does not award credibility. A self-reported AMACS-backed capability remains self-reported until separate evidence/verification rules establish otherwise.

Valid badge sources include:

- profile or account completion;
- authoritative identity evidence;
- platform-observed action;
- configured activity thresholds;
- recipient-confirmed or verified transactions;
- standardized counterparty confirmation;
- platform administrator action; and
- authorized verified-organization endorsement.

## Interpretation, assertion and verification boundary

Keep these distinct:

```text
AMACS concept
≠ interpretation candidate
≠ organization capability assertion
≠ evidence submitted
≠ evidence/capability verified
≠ credibility earned
≠ RFx qualification
```

Interpretation confidence, retrieval rank, model output or participant acceptance cannot be displayed as a credibility seal, verified capability or procurement-readiness status.

## Verification

Platform administrators determine Organization Verified status using accepted organizational identity evidence and identifiers. Verification does not guarantee performance, financial condition, capability for a specific engagement or eligibility for a specific RFx.

Claim authority to manage an organization is a separate prerequisite and business relationship and must not be represented as Verification.

## Profile Complete

Profile Complete is an Active credential or state produced automatically when required identity, contact, capability, geography, visibility and role fields remain complete.

Profile Complete confirms required profile structure, not the truth or independent verification of every capability claim.

## Commercial neutrality

Membership, Founder status, sponsorship and payment cannot make an organization more trustworthy, experienced, verified, endorsed or qualified. A free organization may earn substantive credibility; a paid organization may have none.

Commercial entitlement also cannot improve AI interpretation confidence, convert a suggestion into an assertion or influence legitimate AMACS matching.

## Matching

Credibility does not ordinarily influence RFx search or ranking. It may be used only when an issuer explicitly defines a legitimate required, preferred or informational credential for that RFx.

Capability match, interpretation suggestion and credibility remain separate dimensions. A potential match is not a qualification or endorsement.

## Endorsements

Only currently Verified organizations may issue MVP broad organizational endorsements. Endorsements record the endorser, target, authorized issuing user, type, internal rationale or notes, lifecycle and status. They can be withdrawn, suspended or revoked according to governance.

An endorsement is the endorser's assessment; it is not a guarantee by RFxchange and does not verify every organization claim.

## Anti-gaming

Credibility calculations should protect against self-dealing, duplicate events, reciprocal farming, endorsement rings, low-value transaction splitting and minimum-sample problems. Invalidated or reversed activity must be capable of removing its contribution without erasing history.

AI-generated or repeated interpretation activity cannot count toward participation, experience, trust or outcome badges merely because it consumes platform actions.

## Administration

Authorized admins may verify, deny, award, correct, suspend, restore, revoke, invalidate and review appeals according to permission and scope. Every administrative action creates durable audit evidence; administrative changes do not erase history.

## Public and private boundary

Public profiles show eligible active badges and approved explanations. Raw performance rates, private feedback, evidence documents, calculations, AI interpretation provenance, appeals, investigations and administrator notes remain private or restricted.
