# RFxchange Credibility System Context

## Core principle

Credibility belongs to the **organization**, not individual users. The MVP does not expose a public numerical trust score.

Credibility is communicated through badges backed by auditable credibility events.

## Five credibility families

1. **Verified** — independently established organizational facts.
2. **Active** — meaningful platform participation/contribution.
3. **Experienced** — accumulated RFx, transaction and completed-interaction history.
4. **Trusted** — behavior demonstrated through qualifying interactions and standardized counterparty confirmation.
5. **Endorsed** — broad organizational endorsement issued by an authorized Verified organization.

Recognition markers such as Founding Organization are separate and do not establish substantive credibility.

## Evidence rule

No substantive badge is awarded merely because an organization makes an unsupported claim. Valid sources include:
- profile/account completion,
- authoritative identity evidence,
- platform-observed action,
- configured activity thresholds,
- recipient-confirmed/verified transactions,
- standardized counterparty confirmation,
- platform administrator action,
- authorized verified-organization endorsement.

## Verification

Platform administrators determine Organization Verified status using accepted organizational identity evidence/identifiers. Verification does not guarantee performance, financial condition, capability for a specific engagement or eligibility for a specific RFx.

Claim authority to manage an organization is a separate prerequisite/business relationship and must not be represented as Verification.

## Profile Complete

Profile Complete is an Active credential/state produced automatically when required identity, contact, capability, geography, visibility and role fields remain complete.

## Commercial neutrality

Membership, Founder status, sponsorship and payment cannot make an organization more trustworthy, experienced, verified, endorsed or qualified. A free organization may earn substantive credibility; a paid organization may have none.

## Matching

Credibility does not ordinarily influence RFx search/ranking. It may be used only when an issuer explicitly defines a legitimate required/preferred/informational credential for that RFx.

## Endorsements

Only currently Verified organizations may issue MVP broad organizational endorsements. Endorsements record the endorser, target, authorized issuing user, type, internal rationale/notes, lifecycle and status. They can be withdrawn, suspended or revoked according to governance.

An endorsement is the endorser's assessment; it is not a guarantee by RFxchange and does not verify every organization claim.

## Anti-gaming

Credibility calculations should protect against self-dealing, duplicate events, reciprocal farming, endorsement rings, low-value transaction splitting and minimum-sample problems. Invalidated/reversed activity must be capable of removing its contribution without erasing history.

## Administration

Authorized admins may verify, deny, award, correct, suspend, restore, revoke, invalidate and review appeals according to permission/scope. Every administrative action creates durable audit evidence; administrative changes do not erase history.

## Public/private boundary

Public profiles show eligible active badges and approved explanations. Raw performance rates, private feedback, evidence documents, calculations, appeals, investigations and administrator notes remain private/restricted.
