# RFxchange Commercial Model Context

## Core principle

The model is:

```text
Free to enter → free to participate → pay to do more → selectively monetize advanced/successful activity
```

Network density is part of product value, so core participation must not be crippled by early paywalls.

## Free organization participation

A legitimate organization should be able to establish/claim its presence, appear on the network subject to activation/geography rules, maintain a core capability profile, discover public opportunities, respond through basic workflows, participate in basic referrals/teaming/resource discovery, and earn substantive credibility without paying.

Paid plans may improve productivity, volume, depth, analytics, collaboration or automation. They must not buy access to neutral market legitimacy.

## Founding Organization

The planned Founding concept is organization-level, initially modeled at **$49/month** with a cap of **250 organizations**, subject to approved commercial readiness/terms.

Founding value is expected to come from enhanced tools, early access, structured feedback participation, launch support and recognition—not preferential market treatment.

Founding recognition is historical/commercial recognition, not Verification, Trusted status, qualification or endorsement.

## Official Resource Provider

Official resource-provider status is free after an application/review/verification/provider-approval process. Provider status is an institutional role/status and cannot be purchased.

The provider service profile should represent services/programs, service geography, eligibility, intake, languages/accessibility and availability. Providers may later buy optional operational tooling without payment determining official status.

## Buyers / issuers

Demand creation is strategically valuable. Basic opportunity/RFx issuance should remain available without imposing a percentage fee on RFx awards. Advanced procurement collaboration, templates, approvals, analytics or institutional governance may be monetizable separately.

## Prohibited commercial distortions

Do not implement:
- pay-to-rank capability matching,
- pay-to-verify,
- pay-to-endorse,
- pay-to-qualify,
- commercial status as a substitute for RFx requirements,
- credits required for core actions such as viewing/responding to an RFx, receiving a referral, earning credibility or appearing in legitimate search.

## Managed referral transactions

If RFxchange later manages explicit paid commercial referral transactions, fees may apply under approved terms. This is distinct from ordinary discovery/referrals and distinct from RFx awards.

## Upgrade timing

Commercial conversion should occur after the participant has received or encountered product value. Do not force paid-plan decisions into the minimum activation journey unless a specifically approved entitlement genuinely requires it.

## Architecture implication

Store membership/billing state at the organization level. Keep commercial entitlements separate from organization authority, verification, credibility and neutral market matching.
